# BE-009 — Durable delivery worker and sandbox adapters

Status: **Not started (approval/dependency gated)**

## Objective
Dispatch committed durable intents to local email capture and fake Slack with bounded attempts, immutable recipients/payloads, independent channel outcomes and explicit ambiguous-send recovery.

## Files/components expected — proposed

- `src/Alerts.Infrastructure/Delivery/`: approved SMTP adapter and typed HTTP fake-Slack adapter; destination/credential resolution.

## Approval gates
- Confirm canonical at-least-once attempt semantics, possible duplicate external acceptance, attempt limits/lease/backoff, failure classification and no eventual-delivery guarantee.
- Current reconciled flows/schema/API/security settle `Pending/InFlight/Delivered/RetryScheduled/Failed`, interrupted attempt `OutcomeUnknown` and one dispatcher per channel. Implement these accepted contracts, not historical Sent/Pending-for-retry variants.
- Approve the overview's timeout/retry policy and require downstream bounds, lease/recovery detail and long-Retry-After handling to agree; do not retain a conflicting terminal deferral policy by accident.
- Approve exact SMTP library/version/licence and sandbox receiver tooling before adding dependencies.
- Approve trusted sandbox destinations/profile, immutable recipient identity and handling of revoked/missing destination secrets. Real channel credentials/delivery remain separately gated future work.

## Scoped steps
1. Each channel dispatcher scans its due Pending/RetryScheduled durable work; claim InFlight and record an attempt under the all-writer gate, then commit before external I/O. Preserve one active send per channel rather than imposing a global single-send restriction.
2. Release transaction/gate, send the stored payload to the stored destination identity, then separately commit sanitized outcome/retry state. Use stable intent metadata where supported.
3. Implement approved timeout/retry/Retry-After behavior and terminal failure bounds; preserve each channel's independent state.
4. Recover interrupted/expired claims on startup and during scanning, recording OutcomeUnknown per flows and scheduling RetryScheduled only with remaining attempt budget; otherwise fail terminally with uncertainty visible. No memory queue becomes authoritative.
5. Resolve credential rotation only for the same destination identity; revoked/removed destinations fail explicitly instead of redirecting an old intent.
6. Integrate independent polling and per-channel dispatch workers, scoped contexts and graceful cancellation within the one-host design; capture local email/fake-Slack evidence without production claims.

## Detailed edge-case tests
- Matching manual/synthetic event produces email-only, Slack-only or both selected receipts; nonmatch produces no intent/receipt. One channel's timeout/rejection cannot undo another channel's success or block source polling indefinitely.
- Pending/RetryScheduled work survives restart; Delivered/Failed work is never automatically resent. Claim-before-send failure, crash during send and crash after receiver acceptance before completion record OutcomeUnknown and recover according to the approved contract. Delivered means receiver acceptance, not human reading.
- Ambiguous accepted send may be delivered again; assert durable attempt history and stable intent identity rather than an impossible exactly-once receipt count. Bounded attempts still terminate even across repeated crashes.
- Concurrent scans cannot double-claim an intent; one channel can progress while the other has a send in flight. Expired claims recover without unbounded loops; clock control verifies lease/backoff boundaries. Completion with a stale/wrong claim token cannot overwrite a recovered claim. Cancellation leaves either an acknowledged completion or a durable recoverable attempt, never silent work loss; local in-flight I/O is cancelled/awaited before recovery as specified by the reconciled schema.
- Transient/terminal responses, malformed Retry-After, timeout and credential failure use canonical classifications/bounds. Honor larger valid Retry-After without retrying early; there is no obsolete 24-hour terminal cutoff. Local exponential delay cap does not shorten a valid provider delay.
- Rule edit/pause/delete and destination reprovisioning after event commit cannot alter payload/channel/recipient snapshots. Same-recipient credential rotation works; revoked/changed identity cannot redirect delivery.
- Slow send holds no mutation gate or open DB transaction; another rule/event mutation can commit while send is in flight. Sanitized logs/admin state contain no full webhook URL, credentials or complete sensitive message copy.
- Sandbox messages preserve category, source, simulation/manual provenance, units/window and escaping; unsafe links/header injection are rejected or safely rendered per the canonical format.

## Completion criteria
Actual local email capture/fake-Slack receipts and independent failure outcomes are observed; real SQLite restart/claim/ambiguous-send tests pass with bounded retries. Limits and duplicate risk are documented accurately; shared completion record and independent consistency/security review are complete.

## Excluded work
Mandatory production SMTP/Slack, OAuth/destination verification, distributed leases/broker, manual retry/replay UI/API, digest scheduling and exactly-once guarantees.
